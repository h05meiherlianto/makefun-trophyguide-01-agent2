Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions: story first, side strands second, the long grinds third, cleanup last. Around 200 hours in all. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — the game gives you 15 manual slots plus one autosave — and one untouched save from just before the final mission.

      About this guide

      Names, descriptions, grades, hidden flags and PSN progress targets are the official PlayStation 5 list, as mirrored by Exophase and PSNProfiles and checked on 19 November 2026 — the two sources agree on every name, description and grade. The guides are original writing for this entry. Where a specific step comes from a published guide, that guide is named beside the trophy. Five trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch; their guides say so plainly instead of inventing one. The roadmap and the missable flag are built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order.
